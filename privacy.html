Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use YouNeed, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, profile information, account information, task information, messages, payment transaction information, location information and technical/device information.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide YouNeed services, connect Requesters and Helpers, process payments, facilitate communication, provide support, maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, improve the Platform and comply with legal obligations.

3. Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. We may receive transaction status, amount, date and transaction identifiers. Full payment card details are generally processed directly by payment providers.

4. Location

YouNeed may use location information for location-based features such as finding relevant tasks. Within an active deal, you may voluntarily share a service address, map coordinates, one-time current location, or access notes with the other deal participant only. We do not continuously track your location in the background. Device location permission is requested only when you choose to share.

5. Sharing Information

Certain profile, task and review information may be visible to other users where necessary for Platform functionality. We may also share information with service providers that help operate YouNeed. In-app voice calls, when enabled, are delivered through a voice infrastructure provider without revealing your personal telephone number to the other user.

6. Service Providers

We may use providers for hosting, payments, authentication, maps, notifications, technical support, security, fraud prevention, operational and security logging and (when enabled) encrypted in-app voice connectivity. YouNeed does not currently use a separate product-analytics or advertising measurement SDK in the mobile app.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, taking account of legal, accounting, security and dispute-resolution requirements. Security event records described in section 13 are retained for up to 180 days unless a longer period is required for an active security investigation or legal obligation.

8. Your Rights

Where applicable under GDPR and other law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal information, object to certain processing, request portability, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Children

YouNeed is intended for users aged 16 and older.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date above will be updated when changes are made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data protection requests, contact nuzhnakseniia@gmail.com.

13. Security events and administrator access

To protect accounts and prevent abuse, YouNeed may record security events such as registration and sign-in activity, failed sign-in attempts, device/platform information, browser user-agent where available, and a hashed form of network address (not the raw IP address) together with approximate city-level location derived from that network signal where legally justified. These records are available only to authorized administrators with elevated roles (for example Super Admin or Moderator), not to ordinary support staff by default. Access to security records is itself logged. Shared network signals never automatically prove that two accounts belong to the same person. Collection of these events begins only after this disclosure and the related systems are enabled.

14. In-app voice calls

When in-app voice calling is enabled for a deal, YouNeed connects the customer and provider through a voice infrastructure provider without revealing personal telephone numbers. YouNeed does not record calls and does not store or retain call audio. Audio is used only for the live call session. YouNeed may keep technical call metadata needed to operate the feature for the deal participants — for example call identifiers, deal identifier, caller and callee account identifiers, call status, related timestamps, and an opaque room identifier. This Policy does not set a separate fixed retention period for that call metadata; it is kept only as long as reasonably necessary under section 7, including for dispute handling where applicable. Voluntary location or address information shared inside a deal workspace is visible only to that deal’s participants.

15. Voice task AI assistance

When you choose optional voice task creation, YouNeed may use on-device speech recognition to convert your speech into text. If you continue and ask YouNeed to structure that request into a task, the resulting text (and related language or catalog context needed to structure the task) is sent to YouNeed’s task-assistant service, which uses Google Gemini models to return structured task fields. Audio from that speech recognition step is used to produce the transcript; YouNeed’s task-assistant path processes text, not a retained call recording. Do not include information in a voice task that you do not want processed for this purpose. Google’s processing of the request is subject to Google’s terms for the Gemini API.

How this policy maps to the YouNeed app

  • Hosting & authentication: Supabase (sections 1, 5 and 6).
  • Payments: Stripe Checkout / Stripe Connect; full card numbers are processed by Stripe (section 3).
  • Photos & messaging: User content for tasks, profiles and deals (section 1).
  • Security logging: Optional security-event collection as described in section 13 (enabled only when that system is turned on).
  • Maps: Map views and Apple Maps links using coordinates you set or share (sections 4 and 6).
  • Diagnostics: YouNeed does not currently ship a separate crash-reporting or product-analytics SDK (for example Sentry, Firebase Analytics or Amplitude). Device/OS facilities may still apply outside YouNeed’s own code.
