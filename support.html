Account and login

  • Sign in with the email and password you used to register in the YouNeed app.
  • New accounts may require email confirmation before the first successful sign-in. Check your inbox and spam folder for the confirmation message.
  • If you cannot sign in after confirming your email, contact support with the email address on the account (do not send your password).

Password recovery

  • In the app, open Sign in → Forgot password.
  • Enter your account email. YouNeed sends a reset link to that address.
  • Open the link on your device to set a new password. The app uses the youneed://reset-password deep link.
  • If you do not receive the email, wait a few minutes, check spam, then contact support.

Tasks and offers

  • Customers: create a task with details, location, timing and budget. You can attach photos and, where available, use voice input to draft a task.
  • Helpers: browse tasks, open a listing and submit an offer with your price and terms.
  • Accepting an offer creates a deal between the requester and the helper. The service agreement is between those users.

Deals and payments

  • Use the deal chat and deal workspace to coordinate work, mark completion and confirm results.
  • Where card payments are enabled, customers pay completed card deals through Stripe Checkout in the browser. YouNeed does not collect full card numbers inside the app.
  • Cash or other methods may be agreed for a deal when offered in the app.
  • Platform commission at launch is described in the Terms of Use (0% at launch; any future rate is shown before payment).
  • For payment problems, contact support with the deal reference and approximate time of the attempt — never send full card details by email.

Reporting and blocking users

  • In a deal chat, open the user menu to Report a user and describe the issue.
  • You can Block a user to stop further messaging with them. You may unblock later from the same menu when available.
  • Reports are reviewed by YouNeed moderators. For urgent safety concerns, also contact local emergency services when appropriate.

Privacy and account deletion

  • Read how we handle personal data in the Privacy Policy.
  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete Account, or on the web: Delete account.
  • Web deletion verifies ownership with a one-time email code. Your email address alone is never enough.
