How to request deletion

  • In the YouNeed app: Settings → Delete Account (while signed in).
  • On this page: verify ownership with a one-time email code, then confirm.

How ownership is verified

  • In-app: your active signed-in session (JWT).
  • On the web: a one-time code sent to your account email, then a short-lived verified session.
  • Email address alone never authorizes deletion.

What is deleted

  • Account login and profile (name, phone, city, avatar, business profile fields).
  • Listings, listing photos under your user folder, offers, and tasks you created (where not required for the other party’s retained history).
  • Push tokens, blocks, reports, and identity-verification records tied to your login.
  • Your ability to sign in or access any retained records after deletion.

What may be retained

  • Financial records: transaction id, amount, currency, status, timestamps and payment-processor references may be kept as required for accounting, tax, reconciliation and fraud prevention under applicable law. Exact periods are set by those obligations (to be confirmed with counsel)—not a single blanket timeline for all data. Personal profile links are removed.
  • Disputes: dispute records and any evidence (text today; files if/when stored) may be kept while needed to handle the dispute and related legal claims—not indefinitely. The other participant can still access their side of the dispute history.
  • Reviews: ratings and comments may remain for the other participant’s history with your identity link removed or anonymized.
  • Ordinary chat: conversation text the other participant already received may remain briefly for ongoing deals; messages older than about 24 months and not tied to an open dispute or disputed deal are eligible for automated purge.
  • Stripe Connect account-id mappings (no email/name) may be kept only as long as needed for reconciliation. Category suggestions may remain with the personal link removed.
